# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is local individuals who need their computer equipment diagnosed, repaired, upgraded, assembled, or maintained. Mon Coin PC can also support local professionals.

## Product Purpose

Mon Coin PC gives customers a single point of contact for a computer problem or custom PC project, providing an understandable diagnosis, an appropriate solution, and a price agreed before any work begins. Success means returning reliable, ready-to-use equipment while ensuring the customer clearly understands what was done.

## Positioning

Mon Coin PC stands out first through a personal, direct, and educational relationship. This human proximity is supported by transparent diagnostics, pricing agreed before intervention, and technical expertise built over more than ten years of experience.

## Operating Context

- On-site services around La Ville-aux-Bois-lès-Pontavert.
- Remote assistance available throughout France for compatible software issues.
- The customer describes the issue, the machine, and their availability; Mon Coin PC then establishes a diagnosis and communicates the proposed solution and its cost before intervening.
- Contact begins by phone, email, or quote request form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Hardware and software repair for desktop and laptop PCs.
- Custom PC assembly for gaming, office, or professional use.
- Machine upgrades including RAM, SSDs, graphics cards, and other components.
- Cleaning, maintenance, virus removal, and thermal paste replacement.
- Windows or Linux reinstallation and configuration.
- Component sales and installation.
- Printer and peripheral installation and troubleshooting.
- Data transfer, backup, recovery, and migration.
- Nintendo Switch, PlayStation, and Xbox console maintenance.
- Public “starting from” prices for selected services and quotes for variable work; pricing is agreed before intervention.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Mon Coin PC.
- The business was officially created in April 2025. The founder’s stated experience of more than
  ten years comes from personal practice and helping family and friends before the business opened;
  copy must not imply that the company itself has operated for more than ten years.
- A human, approachable, transparent, and educational voice without unnecessary jargon.
- Human proximity is the primary brand commitment; technical expertise supports it without replacing it.
- Public contact details: `contact@moncoinpc.fr` and `06 66 39 75 17`.
- Core local area: La Ville-aux-Bois-lès-Pontavert and its surroundings.
- Displayed prices, service area, offered services, years of experience, and Google reviews are facts that must be preserved and kept current.

## Evidence on Hand

- More than ten years of stated personal experience assembling, repairing, and optimizing desktop
  and laptop PCs for personal use, family, and friends before creating the business in April 2025.
- A Google Business profile linked from the website, with its rating and review count retrieved through the Google API in production.
- Genuine Google reviews are displayed dynamically when available; reviews embedded in the development code are explicitly local previews and must not be presented as production data.
- The public service and pricing catalogue is located in `src/pages/Services.tsx`.
- The logo is stored in `src/assets/logo.png`, the main photograph in `src/assets/hero-workshop-v2.jpg`, and the social preview in `public/og.png`.
- No additional testimonials, customers, benchmarks, or business results may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Make customers feel that they are dealing with an available, attentive person who remains accountable from start to finish.
2. Explain problems and solutions plainly, without jargon or opacity.
3. Communicate the proposed solution and its cost before any work begins.
4. Adapt every recommendation to the customer's actual need instead of offering a generic response.
5. Support human proximity with verifiable technical expertise and authentic evidence.
